    Delivery

    Thirty minutes, hot

    The clock starts when the order hits the store, not when the rider leaves. Every box travels in a heat-locked bag, so the cheese arrives the way it left the oven.

    Thirty-minute promise

    Order from a store inside your delivery zone and it should be at your door in half an hour. If the kitchen is backed up you will see the honest number before you pay, not after.

    Watch it move

    The tracker updates at five real steps, and each one is a thing a person actually did in the store. No fake progress bar filling up while nothing happens.

    The bag does the work

    Boxes ride in an insulated bag with a heated base plate. Corrugated board wicks the steam so the crust underneath stays firm instead of going soft.
